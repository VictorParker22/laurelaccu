Our approach

The power back in your hands.

A diagnosis or a lab result can feel like something happening to you. Chinese medicine asks a different question: what can you actually do, starting now, to feel like yourself again?

More energy, better sleep, a cycle that makes sense. That's the goal, and it's usually more reachable than people think. Alongside acupuncture and herbs, Laurel talks through real, livable lifestyle changes. Not a total overhaul, just the specific shifts that actually move the needle for your body.

Treatment style

Grounded. Investigative. Collaborative. Understanding.

When you meet, Laurel will check in by asking questions about your whole body system. She wants the full picture before she makes a plan, and she meets you where you're at.

Grounded

Practical plans built around your real life, not an idealized version of it.

Investigative

Careful questions about your whole system, so nothing important gets missed.

Collaborative

You and Laurel decide the next step together. Her job is to make it obvious.

Understanding

Care that starts from where you are and moves in the direction most helpful for you.

About Laurel

Laurel Fruehling, MAScHM

Laurel is in the last months of her Master's in Acupuncture with a Chinese Herbal Medicine specialization at Pacific College of Health and Science in New York City. Her training includes over 1,300 supervised clinical hours, from community acupuncture in East Harlem and the herbal counter at KAMWO to private practice and a hospital rotation at Chang Gung Hospital in Taiwan.

This fall she is at Yinova, one of New York's leading acupuncture and Chinese medicine practices, focusing on women's health and fertility.
