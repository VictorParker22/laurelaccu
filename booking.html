Booking

Where to find Laurel.

Laurel is currently seeing patients at two locations in Manhattan. Choose the one that fits your schedule and follow the steps below to book.

Location one

Pacific College of Health and Science Clinic

Student clinic · Financial District

  • DaysMondays, 1pm–5pm
  • DatesAugust through December 2026
  • Phone(212) 982-4600
  • Address110 William Street, 1st Floor
    (Campus on 19th Floor)
    New York, NY 10038
Call (212) 982-4600

How to book

  1. Call the clinic at (212) 982-4600.
  2. Ask for a Monday afternoon appointment and request Laurel by name.
  3. On the day, go to 110 William Street and check in with the front desk on the 1st floor.
  4. The front desk will give you a badge to enter the clinic space.
The Pacific College campus is on the 19th floor, but the clinic is on the 1st floor. Please arrive a few minutes early to check in and collect your badge.

Location two

Yinova

Reproductive Health Focused Acupuncture · Midtown East

Laurel is deeply honored to be working with Pacific College's graduating interns at Yinova, one of New York's leading acupuncture and Chinese medicine practices specializing in fertility, women's health, and general wellness.

  • DaysTuesdays & Thursdays, 9am–3pm
  • DatesSeptember 29 through December 10
  • Phone212-518-3726
  • Emailappointments@yinova.com
  • Address150 E 58th Street, 6th Floor

How to book

  1. Call 212-518-3726 or email appointments@yinova.com.
  2. Ask for a Tuesday or Thursday appointment between 9am and 3pm and request Laurel.
  3. Arrive at 150 E 58th Street and take the elevator to the 6th floor.
