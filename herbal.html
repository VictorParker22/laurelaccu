Made for you

Every formula is written for the individual.

Two people with the same symptoms may need very different herbs. As you shift, so will your herbal formula. That's the beauty of medicine that is made for you!

A herbal prescription helps to nourish, distribute, or move resources in the body.

Many patients find that combining acupuncture and herbal medicine helps them see progress towards their health goals. In Traditional Chinese Medicine, the two have long been used together whenever possible. They are beautiful friends, each extending a helping hand to the other when it's needed.

Herbal formulas are one of the ways you can support the effects of your acupuncture treatment. But if acupuncture or needles are not your thing, let's start with a personalized herbal formula to take a step in the right direction!

Inside the formula

So what's in these herbal prescriptions?

The Chinese herbal materia medica is made up of 500+ individual herbs, so we have a lot to choose from. All different parts of the plants are used: flowers, leaves and roots, each prepared and harvested at specific times.

Every herb in the materia medica has been carefully studied since antiquity to understand its safety, preparation, and specific effects on the channels and organs of our bodies. In our formulas, the herbs are carefully selected to work with each other. Simple formulas can contain 2 to 4 herbs, while some formulas have 14 or more.

When herbs help most

What health concerns do I find herbs especially helpful for?

Stubborn issues

If you have held off on herbs while getting regular acupuncture treatments and things are not changing the way we would expect, this is an important time to consider herbs. Acupuncture is really good at redistributing the resources your body already has, encouraging qi and blood to flow where they're needed most. If your resources are low or "deficient," this is where herbs can really shine to support you from within.

Chronic issues

These often need the helping hand of herbal formulas. Things have often taken years to become the pattern that they are. Herbs are powerful adaptogens, tightening where needed and loosening where needed to effectively shake up what may be stuck.

Laurel's specialty with herbs

I see herbs shine in two lights: reproductive health and digestive health.

In reproductive health, I use herbs to support menstrual regularity, fertility, and all the lovely symptoms many experience every month with PMS.

In digestive health, I work with those who have bloating, uncomfortable bowel habits, acid reflux, and other discomforts that come with a gut that isn't functioning smoothly.

Digestive health and reproductive health are two areas that are deeply connected to the body's overall balance, which is why herbs can be such a good fit.
